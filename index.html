I'm Arya — half game designer, half software engineer, and a Pokémon fan since before I could spell "Pikachu." Somewhere along the way, years of theorycrafting comps and inventing my own Pokémon turned into an actual habit of finishing what I start, which is how PokeFight happened: a full Pokémon autobattler I designed and built solo, unit by unit, ability by ability, tuned against real players instead of just my own gut.


Professionally, I work as a software engineer on an enterprise tax-compliance platform, where I translate regulatory requirements and client needs into scoped, production-ready features and support them under real deadlines. Outside of both, I'm probably on a volleyball court, out on a trail, attempting a new recipe, or getting supervised by my cat, Diana!
